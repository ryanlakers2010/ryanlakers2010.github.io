★★★★★ 5.0

Northern Lights (His Dark Materials)

by Philip Pullman

Published 1995
Pages 399
Age Group Young Adult

Synopsis & Review

Accompanied by her daemon, Lyra Belacqua sets out to the freezing North to rescue her kidnapped best friend, Roger, and other missing children from the mysterious 'Gobblers'. Along the way, she uncovers dark truths about Dust, parallel worlds, and her own destiny.

Pullman weaves a masterpiece of imagination and philosophical depth. The world-building is intricate, characters are fiercely memorable, and the pacing keeps you captivated from the opening pages at Jordan College to the staggering climax in the Arctic snows.
